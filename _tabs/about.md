# About Me

Hello, I'm **DeepThinkJo**.

I'm an undergraduate student studying Software Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University, with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

I'm fascinated by how intelligent systems work, how mathematical principles make them possible, and how we can turn those ideas into practical technologies.

My goal is to become a **Machine Learning Engineer and Researcher** who can understand, design, and build the core technologies behind intelligent systems.

## Research Interests

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and intelligent systems.

My current areas of interest include:

- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning** — Understanding the principles and architectures behind modern learning algorithms.
- **Computer Vision** — Enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information.
- **Video Understanding** — Learning how AI systems can perceive temporal information and understand dynamic environments.
- **World Models & Robotics** — Exploring how intelligent systems can understand and interact with the physical world.
- **Efficient AI** — Making powerful AI models more efficient, lightweight, and practical for real-world applications.

These interests continue to evolve as I explore new ideas, study foundational concepts, and build projects.

## What I Do

I believe that learning becomes meaningful when theory meets practice.

I spend my time studying the mathematical foundations of AI, exploring computer science, implementing machine learning algorithms, and experimenting with ideas through personal projects.

On this blog, I document that journey through:

- **AI & Machine Learning Theory** — Exploring the mathematical, statistical, and computational foundations of AI.
- **Machine Learning Practice** — Implementing algorithms, conducting experiments, and building projects.
- **Mathematics for AI** — Studying linear algebra, calculus, probability, and optimization.

Rather than simply recording what I learn, I aim to understand why things work and share the insights I gain along the way.

## Philosophy

### Think Deeply. Build Thoughtfully.

I believe that understanding *why* something works is just as important as knowing *how* to use it.

Technology evolves rapidly, and tools and frameworks constantly change. However, the fundamental principles behind them provide a foundation for understanding new technologies and creating something of our own.

I want to go beyond using existing tools. I want to understand the underlying principles, reconstruct ideas from the ground up, and eventually build technologies that make a meaningful difference.

This blog is a place where I explore those ideas, document my growth, and share what I discover.
